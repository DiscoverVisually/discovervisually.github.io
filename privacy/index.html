Privacy,
plainly stated.

We keep this simple because nobody enjoys finding surprises in a privacy notice.

The Reader List.

If you join the Reader List, we ask for one thing: your email address. We do not ask for your name, birthday or other personal details, and we do not build a reader profile about you. We use your email only to send occasional Discover Visually book news, sample pages and release updates.

The signup form is delivered through FormSubmit, which forwards the address you enter to Discover Visually by email. We keep it only for the Reader List and do not sell or share it for advertising.

Leaving is easy.

Every Reader List email will include an unsubscribe option. You can also email hello@discovervisually.com at any time and ask us to remove your address. No explanation needed.

Hosting and technical data.

This website is hosted by GitHub Pages. Like most hosting providers, GitHub may process limited technical information required to deliver and protect the service, such as IP addresses and request logs, under its own privacy terms.

Links to Amazon and other websites.

Book purchase buttons open Amazon in a new tab. Any information you provide after leaving this site is governed by the privacy and purchasing terms of that destination website.

Questions or deletion requests.

Write to hello@discovervisually.com. We will remove your Reader List address when you ask. Last updated: August 5, 2026.
