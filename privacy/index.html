Privacy,
plainly stated.

This page explains what happens when you browse Discover Visually and follow links to third-party retailers.

Information collected on this site.

Discover Visually does not currently offer accounts, checkout or an active mailing-list form on this website, and does not directly request personal information from visitors.

Hosting and technical data.

This website is hosted by GitHub Pages. Like most hosting providers, GitHub may process limited technical information required to deliver and protect the service, such as IP addresses and request logs, under its own privacy terms.

Links to Amazon and other websites.

Book purchase buttons open Amazon in a new tab. Any information you provide after leaving this site is governed by the privacy and purchasing terms of that destination website.

Updates.

This notice will be updated if Discover Visually introduces forms, analytics, accounts or other features that change how visitor information is handled. Last updated: August 5, 2026.
