Frequently asked

Before your
first mission.

Do kids need Battleships puzzle experience?+

No. A training room explains the grid, fleet pieces and deduction rules, then leads the reader through a complete practice puzzle.

Are the historical missions based on real events?+

Yes. Missions follow actual events and settings from the Pacific War. The fleet puzzles are original fictional deductions inspired by history, not claims about precise historical ship positions.

Does the difficulty increase?+

Yes. Islands, hidden row and column totals, and field reports appear gradually with explanations when new clue types are introduced.

Are answers included?+

Yes. There is a full answer key for all missions, together with officer registration, a certificate of service, debriefs and family discussion prompts.

Is it useful for teachers and homeschooling?+

It works as a screen-free activity to complement Pacific War history and critical thinking lessons for ages 8–12.
