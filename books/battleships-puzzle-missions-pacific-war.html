Frequently asked

Before your
first assignment.

Do we need to know Battleships already?+

No. The Training Room explains the five rules with illustrations and a step-by-step solved chart. New clue types are explained as they appear.

Is this the two-player guessing game?+

This is a single-player logic puzzle book. Use row and column totals, given ship pieces and field reports to deduce where the ships belong.

How much history is included?+

The 26 missions follow the Pacific War from Pearl Harbor to Tokyo Bay. Historical briefings, maps and mission debriefs give each puzzle its setting.

Does a child need a screen or app?+

No. The book is a pencil-and-paper activity for readers ages 8–12, with full-color illustrations and progressively harder missions.

Who guides the missions?+

Admiral Nimitz and Admiral Spruance are among the historical guides. Readers begin with Officer Registration and finish their journey with a Certificate of Service.
