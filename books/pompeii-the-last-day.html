Frequently asked

Before you
enter the city.

Is the book frightening or graphic?+

No. The eruption is presented with real tension and historical honesty, but there is no gore or graphic depiction of death. The treatment is designed for readers ages 8–12.

Is this fiction or nonfiction?+

It is visual nonfiction told through an immersive mission-like journey. The first-person viewpoint helps readers experience the setting while maps, timelines, science and archaeology explain the evidence.

Can it work for school or homeschooling?+

Yes. Its Roman daily-life content, volcano science, maps and archaeology make it a useful enrichment book or starting point for a project.

Is it only about the eruption?+

No. Readers see Pompeii before, during and after the disaster—from homes, bakeries and baths to excavation and the preserved city today.

Is Pompeii part of a series?+

Yes. It is Book One in History Hunters, alongside Hindenburg: The Final Flight and I Worked for Abraham Lincoln.
