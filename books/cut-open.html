Does it read like a textbook?

No. The explanations are short and visual-first, but the engineering ideas are real and the language does not talk down to the reader.

Is it only for kids?

It is designed for ages 8+, but the subjects and cutaways are detailed enough to interest many adults too.

What makes it different?

Each subject is treated as a working system: location, function, process and connection are shown together.
