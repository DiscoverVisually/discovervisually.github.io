Is this a romantasy novel?+

No. It is a visual guide, trope map, reader companion and gift book about fantasy romance and the culture around it.

Does it include reading recommendations?+

Yes. Reader path sections help fans explore books by dragons, fae courts, witches, vampires, magical academies, pacing, spice level and more.

Is it an official guide to another series?+

No. This is an unofficial, independently created commentary guide. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by any author, publisher, platform or book series mentioned.
